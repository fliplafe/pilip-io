Satellite Zoning & VRA Service

Satellite-driven precision-agriculture service. Field polygon in, prescription out: a multi-year Sentinel-2 productivity map, management zones with inter-year stability, control lines for on-farm trials, and a prescription-ready split. Zones are post-processed to the farm's machinery; farms without VRA equipment get a one-line split, or none if the field is uniform. Harvester yield data then scores the trial strips against the zones. 2,500+ production runs — private for now.
